# 1. Welcome to Asa: How to Think About This Software

Welcome! If your church or ministry recently began using this software, you might be wondering: *"Why can't we just stick to sticky notes, emails, and phone calls?"* 

When multiple ministries share a church campus, things get lost in the shuffle:
- Two groups book the Fellowship Hall on the same Tuesday evening.
- Someone assumes the communion cups were ordered, but nobody actually placed the order.
- A volunteer steps up to help with Vacation Bible School, but they never receive their checklist.
- Emails get buried in overflowing inboxes.

**Asa** is our church’s central digital bulletin board, supply closet ledger, and organizing binder all in one clean place.

---

### The Physical Church Office Metaphor

If computers make you nervous, don’t think about databases, software, or clouds. Picture a traditional church workroom:

| Physical Church Office Item | What It Is in the Software | Why It Helps You |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A Three-Ring Binder** | **A Project** | Gathers everything for a single ministry or event (e.g., *"Easter Sunday 2026"* or *"Facilities & Grounds"*). |
| **Tabbed Color Dividers** | **Sections** | Breaks your binder into stages like *"To Do"*, *"In Progress"*, *"Waiting on Approval"*, and *"Completed"*. |
| **An Index Card / Action Slip** | **A Task** | One specific job (e.g., *"Order 500 palm branches"*), listing who is doing it, when it is due, and notes. |
| **A Sticky Note Pad** | **Personal Tasks** | Your private scratchpad for reminders only you can see (e.g., *"Call Pastor Dave back at 2 PM"*). |
| **The Supply Room Clipboard** | **Inventory** | A room-by-room count of coffee, napkins, candles, and batteries so we never run out on Sunday. |
| **The Fellowship Hall Sign-up Sheet** | **Meetups** | Allows volunteers to pick open volunteer slots or bring a potluck dish with one tap on their phone. |
| **The Secretary's In-Box Tray** | **Inbox** | Shows notifications when someone gives you a task or replies to your question. |

---

### The Golden Rule: "You Cannot Break It!"

> [!NOTE]  
> **A Reassuring Promise for Beginners:**  
> You cannot accidentally erase the church database by clicking around. Every change is recorded, every completed task can be un-checked, and every deleted item goes into a safety trash bin where it can be restored with a single click. Click freely, explore with curiosity, and don't worry!

---

### How to Sign In for the First Time

1. **Check Your Email:** Your church administrator will send you an invitation email with a link to set up your account.
2. **Open Your Web Browser:** Click the link in your email (or type your church's web address into Google Chrome, Safari, Microsoft Edge, or Firefox).
3. **Set Your Password:**
   - Choose a password you can remember easily (write it down in your personal password notebook if that helps!).
   - Re-type the password to confirm it.
   - Click the green or blue button that says **"Set Password & Sign In"**.
4. **Bookmark the Page:** Press `Ctrl + D` on Windows (or `Command + D` on Mac) to bookmark the page so you can return to it anytime with one click.

---

When you log in, your screen is divided into three friendly areas:


1. **The Left Sidebar:** Your main filing cabinet — switch between tasks, projects, calendar, or supply lists.
2. **The Top Bar:** The Search Bar (find anything by typing a word) and your Profile icon.
3. **The Main Stage:** Where your actual work happens. Click any task and a detail drawer slides out.

# 2. Roles & Permissions: Who Can Do What?

Not everyone in the church needs to see or edit every single item. For example, a youth ministry volunteer does not need to balance the church budget, and a guest bringing cookies to a potluck doesn't need a staff login.

The software has **four distinct access profiles**:

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────┐
                  │          ADMINISTRATOR        │
                  │   Full access across church   │
                  └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                  │
                  ┌───────────────▼───────────────┐
                  │            MANAGER            │
                  │  Leads teams, projects, stats │
                  └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                  │
                  ┌───────────────▼───────────────┐
                  │       USER / TEAM MEMBER      │
                  │  Staff & active church workers│
                  └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                  │
                  ┌───────────────▼───────────────┐
                  │         OUTSIDE GUEST         │
                  │ No login needed (RSVP / Form) │
                  └───────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### The Four Access Profiles

#### 1. Administrator (The "Master Key")
- **Who this is for:** Senior Pastor, Executive Pastor, Office Administrator, or Church IT Coordinator.
- **What they can do:**
  - View and edit **every project** and task across the entire church.
  - Create new user accounts, deactivate accounts, and reset passwords.
  - Form new Teams and adjust the church Org Chart.
  - Build reusable Workflow Templates for church-wide use.
  - Access the **All Trash** safety vault to restore anything anyone accidentally deleted.
  - View church-wide executive dashboards and the Executive Pastor (XP) financial hub.

#### 2. Manager (The "Department Head")
- **Who this is for:** Worship Director, Facilities Director, Children's Ministry Director, Youth Pastor.
- **What they can do:**
  - Create, organize, and manage projects within their ministry area.
  - View a dedicated **Team Dashboard** showing who is overloaded, what is due this week, and what is overdue.
  - Add or remove members within the teams they lead.
  - Reassign tasks, adjust due dates, and oversee ministry goals.

#### 3. User / Team Member (The "Faithful Worker")
- **Who this is for:** Ministry staff, key committee members, teachers, and regular volunteers.
- **What they can do:**
  - Access any project they have been invited to join.
  - Manage their own **My Tasks** list and private **Personal Tasks** scratchpad.
  - Check off tasks as completed, leave comments, and attach files.
  - Log supply counts in the **Inventory** module.
  - Vote on meeting dates and claim volunteer roles in **Meetups**.
  - Restore items they personally deleted from **My Trash**.

#### 4. Outside Guest (The "No Login Required" Visitor)
- **Who this is for:** Congregation members, parents, contractors (like an HVAC repair technician), or one-time volunteers.
- **What they can do:**
  - Submit public **Intake Forms** (e.g., requesting a room setup or submitting a prayer request).
  - Open a secure **Guest Link** sent via email/text to view a specific task.
  - Click a 1-tap **✓ Accept & Confirm** or **Decline** button for a volunteer assignment without creating an account.
  - Sign up for a potluck slot or vote on meeting times.

# 3. Your Daily Personal Workspace

When you begin your work day, you do not need to hunt through dozens of projects to figure out what you need to do. Your personal workspace gathers everything relevant to you in four simple views:

```
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  YOUR DAILY 3-STEP ROUTINE                                    │
  │                                                              │
  │  1. Check INBOX (Top left) ➔ Clear new notifications         │
  │  2. Open MY TASKS          ➔ Review what is due today        │
  │  3. Check CALENDAR         ➔ Look ahead at the week          │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### 1. The Inbox: Triage Without Email Clutter

Click **Inbox** at the top of your sidebar.

Your Asa Inbox is **not** an email inbox. You will never receive spam here. It only notifies you when something directly affects your work:
- Someone assigns a new task to you.
- Someone mentions you in a comment (e.g., *"@Sarah, do we have enough bulletins?"*).
- An intake form you manage was submitted by a church member.
- A reminder fires for an upcoming deadline.

#### How to Process Your Inbox
1. Click on any unread notification. The task or item will open immediately on your screen so you can see what happened.
2. After reviewing it, click the small checkmark icon (**"Mark as Read"**).
3. If you have several notifications and want a fresh slate, click **"Mark All as Read"** at the top right.

> [!TIP]  
> **Peace of Mind:** Think of your Inbox like a mail sorter. Once you’ve looked at a letter, you file it away. Clearing your inbox doesn’t delete the task—it just clears your notification!

---

### 2. My Tasks: Your Personal Action List

Click **My Tasks** in the sidebar.

This is your master to-do list. It automatically pulls every task with your name on it from every single church project and organizes them into neat buckets:
- **Overdue:** Tasks whose due date has passed.
- **Due Today:** What you need to finish before heading home.
- **Upcoming (Next 7 Days):** Things on your horizon.
- **Later:** Long-term tasks.

#### Checking Off a Task
When you finish a task, click the circular checkbox next to its title.
- The task will strike through and celebrate with a soft animation.
- It will automatically move to your **Completed** list.
- Anyone else on that project will instantly see that the task is finished!

---

### 3. Personal Tasks: Your Private Digital Scratchpad

Click **Personal Tasks** in the sidebar.

Have you ever wanted to write down a quick to-do, but didn't want to create an entire formal project for it? That’s what **Personal Tasks** is for:
- **Completely Private:** Nobody else—not even the church administrator or senior pastor—can see your personal tasks.
- **Great for:** Reminders like *"Call plumber for parsonage"*, *"Buy thank-you card for Mary"*, or *"Review sermon scripture notes"*.
- You can give your personal tasks due dates, priorities, and notes, exactly like project tasks.

---

### 4. Calendar View: Visualizing Due Dates

Click **Calendar** in the sidebar.

The Calendar shows you a clean, monthly or weekly layout of all your upcoming church commitments:
- **Color-Coded by Priority:** Urgent tasks appear in bright red/amber; normal tasks appear in calm slate or blue.
- **Filter Bar:** At the top of the calendar, you can filter by project, priority, or person.
- **Rescheduling Made Easy:** If you need to push a task to next Tuesday, simply drag and drop its box from today's square to Tuesday's square!

# 4. Projects & Tasks: The Core Engine

Projects and Tasks are the heartbeat of our church workflow. 

---

### What Is a Project?

A **Project** is a collection of tasks working toward a common goal or managing an ongoing ministry area. 

Examples of great church projects:
- **Event Projects (One-Time):** *"Vacation Bible School 2026"*, *"Fall Craft Fair"*, *"Easter Cantata"*.
- **Ongoing Ministry Projects:** *"Weekly Bulletin & Worship Prep"*, *"Facility Maintenance"*, *"Deacon Care Ministry"*.

#### Creating a New Project
1. Click **All Projects** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the blue button at the top right: **"+ New Project"**.
3. Type a clear name (e.g., *"Thanksgiving Food Drive"*).
4. (Optional) Choose a project folder or apply a pre-built template.
5. Click **"Create Project"**.

---

### The Four Ways to Look at a Project

Every person's brain works differently! Inside any project, look at the top header bar. You will see four view tabs:

```
   [ 📋 List View ]    [ 📌 Board (Kanban) ]    [ 📊 Spreadsheet (Grid) ]    [ 📈 Dashboard ]
```

#### 1. List View (The Traditional Checklist)
- Looks like a standard paper checklist.
- Grouped by sections (e.g., *"Planning"*, *"Execution"*, *"Wrap-Up"*).
- Best for step-by-step sequential tasks and reading long notes.

#### 2. Board View (The Sticky Note Corkboard / Kanban)
- Columns represent progress stages: **To Do**, **In Progress**, and **Done**.
- Tasks appear as physical cards.
- To update a task's status, simply drag the card from "To Do" and drop it into "In Progress" or "Done"!
- Perfect for visual learners.

#### 3. Grid View (The High-Speed Spreadsheet)
- Looks and acts just like Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
- Lets you edit titles, assignees, priorities, and dates rapidly across dozens of tasks without clicking into each one.
- **Superpower:** You can copy (`Ctrl+C`) and paste (`Ctrl+V`) across cells, or drag down to fill values! (See power-user shortcuts in Chapter 15).

#### 4. Dashboard View (Project Statistics)
- Displays pie charts and progress bars showing how close the project is to completion.
- Shows how many tasks are done, in progress, or waiting.

---

### Anatomy of a Task (Step-by-Step Breakdown)

When you click on any task, a detail drawer slides out from the right side of your screen:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  TASK DETAIL PANEL                                          [X] │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  [  ] Order communion juice and wafers                          │
│                                                                 │
│  Assignee:      [ 👤 Pastor Mark  ▼ ]                           │
│  Due Date:      [ 📅 Oct 15, 2026 ▼ ]   Start: [ 📅 Oct 10 ]    │
│  Priority:      [ 🟡 Medium       ▼ ]                           │
│  Status:        [ ⏳ In Progress   ▼ ]                           │
│  Tags:          [ + Add Tag (e.g., Worship, Supplies) ]         │
│                                                                 │
│  Description:                                                   │
│  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐  │
│  │ Please verify that gluten-free wafers are included.       │  │
│  │ Vendor contact number: 555-0199.                          │  │
│  └───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  │
│                                                                 │
│  Subtasks (Checklist):                                          │
│  [x] Check inventory in kitchen cabinet                         │
│  [ ] Place order with Christian supply store                    │
│  [ ] Confirm delivery arrival with church secretary             │
│                                                                 │
│  Activity & Comments:                                           │
│  💬 Sarah: "Left message with vendor on Monday."                │
│  [ Write a reply...                                        ]   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Title:** A clear, action-oriented sentence (e.g., *"Test sanctuary microphones"* rather than just *"Mics"*).
2. **Assignee:** The single person primarily responsible for making sure this job gets done.
3. **Due Date & Start Date:** The deadline. Setting a start date lets you plan work that takes several days.
4. **Priority:**
   - 🔵 **Low:** Do when you have spare time.
   - 🟢 **Medium:** Standard everyday task.
   - 🟠 **High:** Needs attention soon.
   - 🔴 **Urgent:** Critical deadline or obstacle.
5. **Description:** The full story, instructions, phone numbers, or links needed to do the job.
6. **Subtasks:** Smaller checklist items inside the main task.
7. **Comments:** A running conversation thread between team members. Typing `@` followed by someone’s name will send them a notification!

---

### Advanced Task Superpowers

#### 1. Multi-Homing (Putting One Task in Two Places at Once)
Sometimes a task belongs to two different teams. For example:
- Task: *"Set up 50 folding chairs in gym"*
- Belongs to: **Facilities Team** AND **Youth Lock-In Project**.
- In Asa, you can add a task to **multiple projects** without creating duplicates. When someone marks it done in the Facilities project, it is automatically marked done in the Youth project too!

#### 2. Task Dependencies: "Wait on Me First"
If you cannot do Step B until Step A is finished, use Dependencies:
- Open the task, scroll to **Dependencies**, and select the blocker task.
- If *"Print bulletins"* depends on *"Finalize sermon title"*, the system will display a small lock icon alerting you that the sermon title must be finished first.

#### 3. Repeating Tasks (Recurring Work)
Churches have lots of tasks that repeat every week or month. Open any task and check **"This task repeats"**:
- **Frequency:** Daily, Weekly (e.g., *"Every Friday morning"*), Monthly, or Yearly.
- **Repeat Style (Very Important!):**
  - *Option A: Repeat on schedule regardless of completion:* Good for calendar events like *"Set up sanctuary flowers"*. Even if last week's flower setup was delayed, this Sunday's flowers still need to happen.
  - *Option B: Only create the next one AFTER this one is done:* Perfect for maintenance jobs like *"Replace HVAC air filters every 90 days"*. You don't want three filter tasks piling up if the first one hasn't been changed yet!

#### 4. Guest Links & 1-Click RSVPs (For Outside Helpers)
Need a volunteer or outside contractor to confirm a job, but they don't have a login?
1. Open the task and click **"🔗 Share with Guest"**.
2. Click **"+ New Guest Link"**.
3. Copy the private link and send it via text or email.
4. When the recipient opens the link on their smartphone, they see the task details and two large buttons:
   - **✓ Accept & Confirm**
   - **✗ Decline**
5. When they tap **Accept**, the task automatically updates in your project with their confirmation!

# 5. Advanced Project Tools

Once your team is comfortable with basic lists, these powerful tools will save hours of administrative effort each week.

---

### Custom Fields: Tailoring Projects to Church Needs

Every ministry tracks different details:
- The **Worship Team** wants to track *Key (e.g., C Major)*, *Tempo*, and *CCLI Song #*.
- The **Finance Committee** wants to track *Estimated Cost* and *Receipt Attached*.
- The **Facilities Team** wants to track *Room #* and *Equipment Required*.

#### Adding a Custom Field
1. Open your project and click **"Manage Fields"** in the top header.
2. Click **"+ Add Field"**.
3. Choose the field type:
   - **Text:** Short notes or names.
   - **Number:** Dollar amounts, counts, or headcounts.
   - **Date:** Secondary deadlines or inspection dates.
   - **Dropdown:** A neat menu of options (e.g., *"Sanctuary"*, *"Fellowship Hall"*, *"Youth Room"*).
   - **Checkbox:** Simple Yes/No flags (e.g., *"Background Check Verified"*).
4. These fields will now appear as tidy columns in your List and Grid views!

---

### Color-Coded Tags

Tags are colorful labels you can stick to any task across a project (e.g., `#AudioVisual`, `#NeedVolunteers`, `#UrgentPrayer`, `#Kitchen`).
- Click **"Manage Tags"** in your project header to pick colors and tag names.
- You can filter your entire project by clicking any tag to see only those items.

---

### Public Intake Forms: Letting Anyone Submit Requests

Instead of receiving random texts, phone calls, and hallway conversations, you can create a clean **Public Web Form** that automatically turns requests into tasks in your project!

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  PUBLIC INTAKE FORM (No login required)                         │
│  "Sunday Morning AV / Tech Request"                             │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Your Name:           [ Johnathan Miller                      ] │
│  Your Email:          [ jmiller@example.com                   ] │
│  Event Date:          [ 11/08/2026                            ] │
│  Room Needed:         [ Sanctuary                             ] │
│  Microphones Needed:  [ 3 Wireless Handhelds                  ] │
│  Special Notes:       [ Need video projector for slides       ] │
│                                                                 │
│                       [ 📨 SUBMIT REQUEST ]                     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### How to Create an Intake Form
1. Inside your project, click the **Forms** tab.
2. Click **"+ New Form"**.
3. Give your form a name (e.g., *"Facility Usage Request"* or *"Bulletin Announcement Submission"*).
4. Choose which Section new tasks should land in (e.g., *"New Requests (Needs Review)"*).
5. Pick a default person to be notified when a form is submitted.
6. Add your questions (Text boxes, Dates, Dropdowns, or Paragraphs).
7. Click **"Save Form"** and then **"Copy Public Link"**.
8. Paste that link on your church website, in your email newsletter, or email it directly to ministry leaders!

---

### Project Automations: Helpful Robot Assistants

Automations are simple **"If This Happens ➔ Then Do That"** rules that handle routine chores for you:

| When This Happens (Trigger) | What the Robot Does (Action) |
| :--- | :--- |
| When a task is marked **"Done"** in the *"Design"* section... | ➔ Automatically move it to the *"Printing"* section. |
| When a new task lands in the *"Facilities"* project... | ➔ Automatically assign it to our Facilities Director. |
| When a task's due date is **2 days away**... | ➔ Change its priority to **High** and alert the assignee. |

To create a rule, open your project, click **Automations**, click **"+ New Rule"**, and assemble your sentence using the friendly dropdown menus!

---

### Reusable Workflow Templates

Why reinvent the wheel every time your church hosts a wedding, funeral, or baptism?
- Administrators can build a master **Workflow Template** that contains the entire checklist, subtasks, and timelines.
- When an event is scheduled, open the project, go to the **Workflow** tab, and click **"Apply Template"**.
- In two seconds, 40 pre-arranged tasks are populated into your project, ready to be assigned!

# 6. Inventory & Supplies Management

Have you ever opened the church kitchen cabinet on Sunday morning only to discover we are completely out of 8-ounce coffee cups? The **Inventory Module** eliminates this frustration forever.

### Why Track Inventory?

# 8. RACI Responsibility Charts: Ending "Who's Doing That?"

Have you ever worked on a major church event where everyone assumed someone else was handling the sound system, and Sunday morning arrived with no audio? 

A **RACI Chart** is a proven, visual chart that ensures total clarity across complex church projects.

---

### What Does RACI Stand For?

| Letter | Word | Plain-English Church Meaning |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **R** | **Responsible** | **"The Doer"** — The specific person with their hands on the tools doing the work. |
| **A** | **Accountable** | **"The Owner"** — The single person who ensures it gets done right and on time. (Only ONE person should be Accountable per step!). |
| **C** | **Consulted** | **"The Advisor"** — Someone with wisdom or expertise who must be asked for input before making decisions. |
| **I** | **Informed** | **"The Loop"** — People who need an update or heads-up once the decision is made, but don't need to do the work. |

---

### Real-World Example: Church Fall Festival

Look at how clear this matrix makes our responsibilities:

| Step / Decision | Pastor David (Pastor) | Karen (Children's Director) | Tom (Facilities) | Sarah (Office Secretary) |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1. Reserve City Park Permit | **I** *(Keep informed)* | **A** *(Accountable)* | **I** | **R** *(Fills out paperwork)* |
| 2. Arrange Carnival Games & Prizes | **I** | **A / R** *(Plans & buys)* | **C** *(Consulted on space)* | **I** |
| 3. Set up Electrical Generators & Tents | **I** | **C** | **A / R** *(Sets up gear)* | **I** |
| 4. Publish Event in Bulletin & Social Media | **C** *(Reviews wording)* | **A** | **I** | **R** *(Prints & posts)* |

*Result:* No arguments, no dropped balls, and no hurt feelings. Everyone knows their lane and feels supported!

# 13. Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A)

### Everyday Basics

#### Q1: "I clicked on something and now my screen looks different. How do I get back home?"
**A:** Simply click **My Tasks** or **All Projects** in the left sidebar. The left sidebar is always there to guide you back to familiar territory!

#### Q2: "Can people from other churches or hackers see our church data?"
**A:** No. Our church has its own completely separate, private, encrypted database. Only authorized accounts created by your church administrator can access your system.

#### Q3: "Do I have to log in on a computer, or can I use my iPhone, iPad, or Android phone?"
**A:** Asa works beautifully on any smartphone or tablet! Open Safari, Chrome, or your phone's browser, go to your church's web link, and log in. You can even tap **"Add to Home Screen"** on your phone to create a dedicated app icon!

---

### Tasks & Projects

#### Q4: "I checked off a task by accident! How do I bring it back?"
**A:** Go to the bottom of the project list or your My Tasks list and look for the **Completed** section. Click the checkbox again, and it will immediately un-complete and return to your active list.

#### Q5: "What is the difference between a Task and a Subtask?"
**A:** A Task is the main job (e.g., *"Host New Member Reception"*). Subtasks are the bite-sized steps required to finish it (e.g., *"1. Buy cookies"*, *"2. Brew punch"*, *"3. Print name tags"*).

#### Q6: "Why can't I see a project that my coworker was talking about?"
**A:** If a project was created as a private project, only members invited to it can see it. Ask your coworker or church administrator to click **"Invite Member"** on the project and add your name!

---

### Volunteers & Outside Communication

#### Q7: "How do I send a checklist to a volunteer who refuses to use computers?"
**A:** Open the project in **List View**, press `Ctrl + P` (or `Command + P` on Mac), and print a crisp, clean paper copy! Asa has a built-in printer stylesheet that hides all buttons and prints a clean, beautiful paper checklist for volunteers who prefer paper.

#### Q8: "Can an outside contractor or wedding coordinator comment on a task without an account?"
**A:** Yes! Open the task, click **"🔗 Share with Guest"**, generate a guest link, and text or email it to them. They can view the task, see the notes, and post a comment directly from their phone without logging in.

Pressing keys on your keyboard can save you dozens of clicks!

- **`Tab` / `Shift + Tab`:** Move horizontally across columns.
- **`Ctrl + C` / `Ctrl + V`:** Copy and Paste text or dates across cells.
- **`Ctrl + D` (Fill Down):** Copies the value from the top cell down across all highlighted rows!
- **`Ctrl + Z`:** Undo last bulk edit.
